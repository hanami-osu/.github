# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Hanami should be a welcoming place to collaborate. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sustained personal attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies in Hanami repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing Hanami.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [me@yorunoken.com](mailto:me@yorunoken.com) with the subject `Hanami conduct report`. Include relevant links or screenshots when possible.

Maintainers may remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
